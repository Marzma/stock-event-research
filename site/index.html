Definitions & coverage

Prices: user-supplied Investing.com NBIS and QQQ CSV exports. 505 common completed sessions from October 21, 2024 to October 26, 2026. October 27 is excluded. The first session has no in-sample prior close and is excluded from daily-return screening.

Return: (close / prior available close − 1) × 100. Strictly greater than 5% is applied before rounding. Prices are not independently verified as dividend-adjusted. Volume inherits K/M rounding in the source.

Relative performance: NBIS return minus QQQ return, in percentage points. This is not risk-adjusted alpha.

Event windows: baseline is the close before the rate-cut announcement; 0, 1, 5 and 20 refer to observed common trading sessions after announcement day. Dates match between the two input files, but were not checked against an independent exchange calendar.

Research: initial date-specific searches were completed for all 118 rally dates on October 27, 2026. Only recorded evidence has been reviewed. Most search results still need article-level verification. Missing records mean incomplete coverage, not absence of news. Reaction dates are left unknown where timing is not established.

Scope: five in-sample cuts, all 25 bps. The September 2024 cut predates the price sample. Expected policy, announcement surprises and pre-event trends are not modeled. The dashboard is an exported MySQL snapshot and does not refresh automatically.
